Armies that fit

The fit score counts which of the defences you placed can hit air or ground, then ranks Army Library entries whose Town Hall range covers this base. It does not know levels, walls, the Clan Castle or the defending heroes, and it is not machine vision: the screenshot is only drawn behind the grid.
